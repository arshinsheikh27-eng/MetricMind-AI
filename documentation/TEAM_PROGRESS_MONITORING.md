# Team Progress Monitoring and Deadline Tracking

## Purpose

As Team Lead, a structured progress-monitoring process is maintained to ensure that assigned project tasks are completed on time and that the MetricMind-AI project progresses according to the planned schedule.

## Team Progress Tracking

A shared Google Sheet is maintained to monitor the team's work and provide visibility into individual and overall project progress.

The tracking sheet contains three sections:

### 1. Daily Task Done

This section records the tasks completed by team members on a daily basis.

It helps the Team Lead:
- Monitor daily progress.
- Identify completed work.
- Maintain visibility of individual contributions.
- Detect gaps or delays in planned work.

### 2. Weekly Task Done

This section records the tasks completed during each week.

It helps in:
- Reviewing weekly progress.
- Comparing completed work with planned objectives.
- Identifying pending activities.
- Preparing for weekly project reviews.

### 3. Weekly Task Assigned

This section records the tasks assigned to each team member for the respective week.

It helps the Team Lead:
- Distribute responsibilities clearly.
- Track ownership of project modules.
- Compare assigned tasks with completed tasks.
- Identify tasks requiring follow-up.

## Continuous Team Follow-up

The Team Lead continuously communicates with team members to obtain updates regarding their assigned tasks and overall project progress.

Regular follow-ups are used to:
- Check the status of ongoing tasks.
- Identify technical or coordination-related blockers.
- Monitor pending work.
- Confirm whether planned tasks are progressing as expected.
- Coordinate dependencies between team members.
- Take necessary action when progress is delayed.

## Deadline Monitoring

Team progress is monitored against the project timeline to ensure that assigned work and integration activities are completed before the submission and review deadlines.

When pending work or dependencies are identified, the Team Lead follows up with the respective member and coordinates the next steps to keep the project on schedule.

## Team Lead Responsibility

The progress-monitoring process helps the Team Lead maintain an overall view of the project rather than focusing only on an individual module.

The objective is to ensure:

- Clear task ownership.
- Regular progress updates.
- Timely identification of blockers.
- Proper coordination between modules.
- Completion of assigned work within the planned timeline.
- Readiness of the complete team for project reviews and final submission.
