\## Key Business Relationships



\### Revenue



Total Revenue represents the sales value generated by an order.



Expected relationship:



`Total Revenue = Units Sold × Unit Price`



\### Cost



Total Cost represents the cost associated with units sold.



Expected relationship:



`Total Cost = Units Sold × Unit Cost`



\### Profit



Total Profit represents the difference between revenue and cost.



Expected relationship:



`Total Profit = Total Revenue - Total Cost`



\### Profit Margin



Profit Margin represents profit as a percentage of revenue.



Expected relationship:



`Profit Margin % = (Total Profit / Total Revenue) × 100`



\### Shipping Days



Shipping Days represents the difference between Ship Date and Order Date.



Expected relationship:



`Shipping Days = Ship Date - Order Date`
