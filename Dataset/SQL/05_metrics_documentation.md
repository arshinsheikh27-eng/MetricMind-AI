# Metrics Documentation

## Total Revenue
Formula: SUM(Total Revenue)

## Total Cost
Formula: SUM(Total Cost)

## Total Profit
Formula: SUM(Total Profit)

## Profit Margin %
Formula: Total Profit / Total Revenue * 100

## Average Order Value
Formula: Total Revenue / Total Orders

## Average Profit Per Order
Formula: Total Profit / Total Orders

## Profit Per Unit
Formula: Total Profit / Units Sold

## Average Shipping Days
Formula: AVG(Shipping Days)
